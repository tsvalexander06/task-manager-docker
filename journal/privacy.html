Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • See everything we hold — it is the journal itself.
  • Export it — the journal exports your whole record, screenshots included, as a single file. No request needed, no charge.
  • Correct it — edit any entry.
  • Delete it — delete entries yourself, or email [[SUPPORT EMAIL]] to have the account and everything in it removed. We do that within 30 days and confirm when it is done.
  • Object or restrict — ask us to stop or limit processing, and withdraw consent where we relied on it.

If you think we have handled your data badly, tell us first — but you can complain to [[SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY — e.g. the ICO at ico.org.uk]].

Security

Your journal is isolated from every other account at the database level, not in the browser: the rules deciding who can read a row live in Postgres, so another customer cannot reach your data even by tampering with the page. Screenshots sit in a private bucket behind short-lived signed links. Everything travels over HTTPS.

No system is perfect. If there is a breach affecting you, we will tell you and the relevant authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

Cookies

The journal sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It stores a sign-in token in your browser so you stay signed in, and remembers preferences such as your chosen currency. Both are necessary for the service to work.

Children

The journal is not for anyone under 18.
