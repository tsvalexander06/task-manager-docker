Risk disclaimer

Read this first

Trading carries a high level of risk and can result in the loss of all of your capital. On leveraged products it can cost you more than you deposit. Do not trade with money you cannot afford to lose, and do not treat anything in this journal as a reason to.

What this product is

Odyssey Journal is a record-keeping tool. You type in trades you have already taken; it stores them, adds them up, and shows you the result. Everything it displays is arithmetic performed on numbers you supplied.

What it is not

  • Not financial advice. Nothing in the journal is a personal recommendation, and nothing in it takes account of your circumstances, objectives or risk tolerance.
  • Not a signal service. It does not tell you what to trade, when to enter, or when to exit.
  • Not a prediction. Every score, rating and chart describes trades you have already taken. None of it forecasts anything.
  • Not a broker. We do not execute orders, hold your money, or connect to your trading account.
  • Not regulated. We are not authorised by [[YOUR REGULATOR — e.g. the FCA]] or any other financial regulator, because a journal does not require authorisation. If you want advice, take it from someone who is authorised to give it.

About the numbers it shows you

Statistics, scores and indices in the journal — including the process score, the mistake score and every win-rate and expectancy figure — are descriptive summaries of what you entered. They are only as accurate as your own record keeping.

Past performance does not indicate future results. A strong figure in the journal means a set of past trades came out well. It does not mean the next one will. A sample of a few dozen trades is small enough that luck dominates skill in any figure computed from it.

The instruments you are likely trading

Risk is not the same across markets:

  • Forex. High leverage, moves around the clock, and turns on policy and politics you cannot forecast. The majority of retail forex accounts lose money.
  • Indices and commodities. Gap through weekends and around economic releases. A stop does not guarantee your exit price.
  • Crypto. Volatile, thinly regulated in many places, and exposed to exchange failure and manipulation as well as price.
  • Options and futures. Complex, dated, and margined. Options can expire worthless; futures can call for more money than you put up.

A stop-loss is not a guarantee. Markets gap, and you can be filled well beyond where you intended to be out.

The numbers are only as good as what you typed

We do not connect to your broker and we cannot check your entries. Every figure comes from what you recorded, including any mistake in it. Reconcile against your broker statements before you rely on a number for anything that matters — certainly before you file tax from it.

Before you trade, not after

If you are getting started, get proper education, practise on a demo account, write a plan, size your risk deliberately, and speak to someone licensed to advise you. A journal helps you see what you did. It cannot make an unprofitable approach profitable.

The service can be unavailable

Outages, faults and data problems happen. Do not make the journal the only place your record exists — it exports everything at any time, and takes automatic restore points. Do not rely on it being reachable at a moment that matters.

Example data

Any sample or demonstration record shipped with the journal is invented for the purpose of showing what the screens look like. It is not a real trading history, not anyone's results, and not a representation of what the product can earn you.

Prop firm accounts

Where you track a funded or challenge account, the rules, targets and drawdown limits you enter are your record of what your provider told you. We do not verify them against your provider and we are not a party to that agreement. Do not rely on the journal to tell you whether you have breached a rule — check with the firm holding the account.

Your decisions are yours

You are solely responsible for every trade you place and for any loss arising from it. To the fullest extent the law allows, we accept no liability for trading losses, whether or not you consulted the journal first, and whether or not it was working correctly at the time. The limits in the terms of service apply.

Tax

The journal is not a tax record and its figures are not prepared to any accounting standard. Do not file from it without checking. Whether trading profits are taxable, and how, depends on where you live — [[JURISDICTION]] in your case, if you are reading this as the operator. Speak to an accountant.

What you are agreeing to
