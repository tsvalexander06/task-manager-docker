The short version

Cancel any time. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not billed again. Whether an already-paid period is refunded is set out below.

Cancelling

Open the billing portal from inside the journal and cancel there. It takes effect at the end of the current period — no email to us, no notice period, no questions.

Refunds

(b) Full refund within 14 days of your first payment, no reason needed; after that, no refunds on part-used periods. (c) Full refund of the current period at any time, on request.]]

If you are a UK or EU consumer

You normally have 14 days to cancel a digital service and get your money back. Because access starts immediately, that right ends once you begin using the service — but only if you agreed to that at checkout. [[Confirm your Stripe Checkout actually collects that agreement. If it does not, the 14-day right applies in full and this section has to say so.]]

When we refund without being asked

  • You were charged after cancelling.
  • You were charged twice for the same period.
  • We ended your account without cause — we refund the unused part.
  • The service was unavailable for an extended period through our fault: [[e.g. more than 72 consecutive hours]].

Asking for one

Email [[SUPPORT EMAIL]] from the address on the account. We reply within [[e.g. 2 working days]]. Approved refunds go back to the original card and take 5–10 days to appear.

Chargebacks

If something is wrong, email us first — it is faster than a chargeback and we will almost certainly just fix it. Raising a chargeback without contacting us suspends the account while the bank investigates.
